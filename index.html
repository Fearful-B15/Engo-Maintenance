Maintenance Report Management System

The Maintenance Report Management System was designed to modernize how Engo manages maintenance tasks across its child and youth care facilities. It replaces manual reporting with a centralized digital platform for submitting requests, tracking progress, and generating reports.

This system enhances communication between staff, caretakers, and maintenance teams, reducing downtime, minimizing risk, and improving safety and comfort for children and staff. It promotes accountability and efficiency through real-time updates and record-keeping.

About Engo: Engo is a respected non-profit welfare organization in the Free State, South Africa, with over a century of service. Originating in 1902, Engo has grown from early child care centers into a province-wide network dedicated to protecting, educating, and nurturing vulnerable children and youth through community-based care programs.
