Dian van Eyk IM WIL Project 2025

Maintenance Report Management System

The Maintenance Report Management System modernizes how Engo manages maintenance tasks across its child and youth care facilities. It replaces manual reporting with a digital platform for submitting requests, tracking progress, and generating reports.

This system improves communication, reduces downtime, and enhances safety for both children and staff by automating workflows and maintaining detailed maintenance records.

About Engo: Engo is a leading non-profit welfare organization in the Free State, South Africa, with over a century of service. It provides child and youth care, family support, and community-based development through a network of care centers.
